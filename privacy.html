Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

TMC Capital Holdings ("TMC," "we," "us") buys residential rental properties in Central Florida. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you request an offer, call, text or email us, we collect what you share, such as your name, phone number, email, property address, and details about the property and your situation.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our site and hosting provider may record basic technical data such as your browser type, device, pages visited and the time of your visit. We may also record which buttons you tap (for example "Call" or "Text") so we can understand how the site is used. We do not collect payment information on this site.

How we use your information

  • To review your property and respond to your request, including preparing an offer if your property qualifies.
  • To contact you by phone, text message or email about your property.
  • To keep records of our conversations and any transaction.
  • To improve our website and understand how people find us.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

Requesting an offer does not obligate you to sell your property.

Text messages and calls

If you submit our form or text us, you agree that we may contact you by call or text at the number you provide, including by automated means, about your property. Consent is not a condition of selling to us. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time, or HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your mobile number or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as our website host, the service that delivers form submissions to our email, and phone and email providers. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • With parties involved in a transaction you choose to move forward with, such as title companies, closing attorneys, lenders, inspectors and insurers.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, property or safety, or that of others.

How long we keep information

We keep information as long as needed to respond to you, complete any transaction and meet legal, tax and record-keeping requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

How we protect information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information you share. No website, email or phone system is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive information such as Social Security or bank account numbers through our website form.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts.
  • Ask us to stop calling or emailing you, to tell you what information we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, by contacting us below. We will respond within a reasonable time, unless we need to keep the information for legal reasons.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Links to other sites

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
