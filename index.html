Questions

Straight answers.

Am I obligated to sell if I request an offer?

No. An offer is just an offer. You can ask questions, compare it with other options or say no.

How do you decide what to offer?

We look at current rents, expenses, the property's condition, needed repairs and comparable sales, then run conservative numbers. We'll walk you through them.

Will every property get an offer?

No. We only make offers on properties that fit what we buy and pass our underwriting. If yours doesn't, we'll tell you plainly.

What happens to my tenants?

Tenants can usually stay in place. Existing leases transfer with the sale.

Are you a real estate agent?

Gory is a licensed Florida real estate sales associate (SL3395484). When TMC buys your property, we're buying for our own account as a principal, not representing you. You're welcome to have your own agent or attorney review any offer.
